HOW THE SUITE FITS TOGETHER

Buy one product, some or all.

  • One way to sign in. Everyone signs in with their work email and a one-time code. Your company decides who has access to which product, and nobody can sign themselves up.
  • Each product stands alone. It works without the others, and keeps working if one is switched off.
  • Data moves only through connections you switch on. An employee’s private HiveRecord item moves only when that employee shares it.

TRUST & GOVERNANCE

Trust is part of
the product.

Enterprise buyers and their people ask these questions first, so the answers are built into how the products work rather than added as policy afterwards.

The employee chooses

In HiveConnect people opt in per activity, and a suggestion is never a connection. HiveRecord belongs to the employee: nobody else sees it unless they share an item, with a named person, until a date they choose.

Explainable by design

Every match, risk band and measured effect shows the evidence behind it in plain language, and HiveImpact grades every number by how strong that evidence is.

Your data stays yours

Each product runs in its own environment with its own tenant boundary. Data moves between Hive products only through connections your organisation switches on, and never between customers.

Designed against real frameworks

ICO guidance on monitoring workers, the EU AI Act and NIST's AI Risk Management Framework shaped the design from day one.

What the designs leave out: individual productivity scoreskeystroke or screen monitoringbackground reading of inboxes or chatsinferred emotion or sentiment
